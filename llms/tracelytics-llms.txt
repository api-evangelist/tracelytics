# Tracelytics

> Tracelytics was a Boston/Providence application-tracing (APM) startup whose full-stack tracing product became AppNeta TraceView after AppNeta acquired the company in June 2012. TraceView was subsequently acquired by SolarWinds in 2016, and AppNeta itself by Broadcom in 2021. Tracelytics no longer operates independently: tracelytics.com no longer resolves (no DNS records) and the historical TraceView Data API host api.tracelytics.com is gone. This profile preserves the company's lineage and its surviving public artifacts for the API Evangelist network.
